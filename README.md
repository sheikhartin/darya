# Darya (دریا)

Darya is a bilingual Persian and English conversation companion. It listens, asks thoughtful questions, and helps you think through what is on your mind.

It works fully offline, runs as a single page with no server, and can be installed as a progressive web app or packaged for mobile.

## What it does

Darya recognizes a wide range of emotional topics, tracks the flow of your conversation, and varies its responses so it does not feel mechanical. It remembers what matters within your current session, offers reflective questions instead of generic prompts, and respects clear boundaries around professional advice and safety.

Persian and English are separate, equally detailed language packs sharing one engine. The language is chosen once at the start of a conversation and stays locked for its duration.

## What it is not

Darya is not therapy and does not diagnose anything. There is no learning and no memory beyond a single browser tab. The site is honest about that limitation.

## How to use it

Open `index.html` in a browser. Choose your language. Start talking. Everything runs locally in your browser with nothing sent to any server.

To install as a PWA, open the site in a modern browser and use the "Add to home screen" option.

## Offline support

After the first visit, the entire app works without an internet connection. A service worker caches every file needed to run the application.

## Themes

Two visual themes are available: ocean and beach. Your theme choice is remembered between visits.
