# Knowledge research and source policy

Last reviewed: 2026-08-21

Darya is fully offline at runtime. Curated answers therefore need a clear
line between durable background knowledge and facts that can change after
release. This document records the source families used for the software,
security, employment, Iran, migration, conscription, history, relationship,
and body-image expansion.

## How sources are used

- Primary law, government, standards, and international-organization pages
  are preferred for rules, official processes, and professional frameworks.
- Academic encyclopedias, museums, and major reference works are used for
  stable history.
- Clinical and public-health sources are used for wellbeing literacy. Darya
  does not diagnose.
- News and community posts may reveal questions people actually ask, but are
  not treated as proof of a legal rule, statistic, or historical claim.
- A side's stated reason for war is labeled as a claim or motive. Explaining
  it does not make all claims true, all conduct lawful, or responsibility
  equal.
- Product prices, wages, exchange rates, visa rules, border conditions,
  opening hours, and individual conscription outcomes are live facts. Darya
  does not freeze them into an offline answer.

## Iranian compulsory military service and international comparison

Iranian service length is not represented as one universal number. Recent
announcements have described variation by posting, local or nonlocal status,
and duty type, while the underlying statute and implementation can change.
The app states reported ranges only to correct the claim that every case is
identical, then directs the user to current official information.

  http://vazifeh.police.ir/index.jsp?p=1&pageid=5174

## Social and community listening

Community discussions were reviewed to find natural questions and pain
points, including career interruption during service, migration tradeoffs,
credential loss after migration, job-search frustration, and the gap between
official duration and individual service experience. Reddit discussions and
comment sections are anecdotal, self-selected, and sometimes politically
charged. They informed test wording only, never a factual answer or legal
claim.

Iranian social-media listening for the people shelf used public Instagram
ranking snapshots (StarNgage and Heepsy, reviewed 2026) only to see which
names people actually type as «فلانی کیه». Follower counts are live facts
and are not frozen into answers. Bios stay descriptive. Political figures
as primary subjects, and legally controversial entertainers, were skipped.

## Review lenses

No separate expert-agent service was available in the development
workspace. Instead, the changes are reviewed independently through these
lenses:

1. Software architecture and regression behavior.
2. Defensive cybersecurity, authorization, and abuse resistance.
3. Iran-aware legal-risk wording for migration and conscription.
4. Historical sourcing, multiple perspectives, and non-equivalence.
5. Employment realism, scam resistance, and no fixed product prices.
6. Wellbeing, coercive-control safety, body image, and crisis escalation.
7. Persian language quality, including Iranian Yeh and Kaf code points.
8. Offline PWA and Capacitor asset parity.
9. Public-figure bios: descriptive only, no gossip, no ranking of private
   lives; social listening chooses who people ask about, not what to claim.

A review pass is not a substitute for a licensed Iranian lawyer, clinician,
historian specializing in every conflict, or current government source. It
is a way to expose conflicting requirements before release.
